Senior Backend Engineer - Platform Team

We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Platform team. You'll be
responsible for designing and maintaining scalable microservices that power our
core product.

Requirements:

- 5+ years of experience in backend development
- Strong proficiency in Python and Go
- Experience with distributed systems and message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ)
- Solid understanding of PostgreSQL and Redis
- Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes
- Experience with AWS or GCP cloud infrastructure
- Excellent communication skills and ability to mentor junior engineers

Nice to have:

- Experience with gRPC and Protocol Buffers
- Contributions to open source projects
- Background in high-throughput, low-latency systems

This is a full-time, remote-friendly position. We offer competitive salary,
equity, and comprehensive benefits.
